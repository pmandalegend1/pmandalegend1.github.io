About

A short introduction

I build ML systems grounded in physical intuition — the kind that fall out of how a sensor actually behaves, how a flight schedule actually propagates, or how a medical image is actually formed. I care less about chasing benchmarks and more about whether a model is doing something a domain expert would recognise as reasonable.

Right now I'm a concurrent research intern at IIT Gandhinagar (piezoresistive sensor pipelines, with Dr. Biswajit Saha) and IIIT Allahabad (medical image translation, with Dr. Shiv Ram Dubey). My first paper — a rotation-aware approach to flight delay propagation — is under conference review.

I'm a U.S. citizen based in India, so I'm equally available for roles in either country, remote or on-site. The fastest way to reach me is email.
